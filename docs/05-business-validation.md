# Business Model and Validation Plan

**Status:** Hypotheses to validate; no market sizing or competitor research has been completed.

## Value proposition hypothesis

For people with disruptive menstrual or pelvic symptoms, the app may reduce the effort of keeping a record and help them communicate symptoms during care visits. For clinicians, a user-controlled, clearly labeled history may make consultation preparation easier.

## Initial market hypothesis

Kenya, English first, with Swahili and low-data support as priorities. Validate smartphone access, language preference, privacy concerns, ability to seek care, and local care pathways across different counties and communities. Do not assume one user segment represents the whole market.

## Alternatives and competitor research

Research existing period trackers, symptom diaries, clinic workflows, paper notes, and local women's-health services. Compare privacy model, offline support, accessibility, clinical boundaries, language, and report usefulness. Record date, evidence, and source for each comparison; no competitor conclusions are asserted yet.

## Validation plan

1. Conduct formative interviews with adults and clinicians; document consent, recruitment, and ethics needs first.
2. Test a clickable prototype with representative users, including low-literacy and low-connectivity contexts where feasible.
3. Measure task completion, time to log, comprehension of non-diagnostic wording, report usefulness, and privacy comfort.
4. Iterate; assess usability before building automated screening.
5. If studying clinical outcomes, prepare a separate protocol, obtain required ethics/regulatory approvals, and involve independent clinical/statistical expertise.

Do not recruit minors or collect research data until applicable ethics, consent/assent, safeguarding, and legal requirements are reviewed.

## Business model hypotheses

Possible future support: grants, university or health-system partnerships, institutional licenses, or sponsored access. Any paid feature requires affordability and equity review. Do not sell personal health data or use sensitive data for advertising. A facility listing must be verified and clearly disclose any commercial relationship.

## Validation metrics

- User can complete core logging and report tasks without assistance.
- Users understand that the app is not a diagnosis.
- Users can identify and control what information a report contains.
- Clinicians find the report legible and appropriately qualified.
- Retention and repeat use are assessed without treating them as evidence of clinical effectiveness.
